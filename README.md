# Montessori Virtual Room

**Step inside a virtual Montessori classroom. Discover the materials, understand their purpose, and explore how they work.**

Montessori Virtual Room is a free resource for curious parents, educators, and families. It brings the classroom closer through spaces you can explore, materials you can manipulate, and explanations you can read or listen to. No account is needed.

## Look around. Follow your curiosity.

Choose an age group and enter its environment. Select a material to learn what it is for, then try the activity at your own pace. Watch a moving example when you need a starting point, or use **Check my work** to understand your arrangement.

Build the Pink Tower, fit cylinders, practice pouring, arrange counters, explore continent shapes, or compare fractions. Every activity offers space to observe, repeat, and try again.

## From a quiet beginning to a wider world

Explore six environments:

- **Nido · 0–18 months** — an introduction for adults to spaces for movement, care, and connection.
- **Toddler Community · 18 months–3 years** — everyday movements and growing independence.
- **Children’s House · 3–6 years** — practical life, sensorial exploration, early mathematics, and geography.
- **Lower Elementary · 6–9 years** — shared exploration, quantities, shapes, and wider questions.
- **Upper Elementary · 9–12 years** — a setting for research, reasoning, and continued material exploration.
- **Adolescent Community · 12–18 years** — an introduction to study, practical work, and community life.

There are currently **15 interactive activities**, shared across the toddler, Children’s House, and elementary environments. The Nido and adolescent environments are explanatory tours.

## Understand the purpose behind the material

Parent and educator notes explain the aims of each material, how it is traditionally presented, and what a child may explore next. Optional narration and demonstrations provide another way to learn.

The experience encourages concentration and repetition, with no timers, scores, or leaderboards. Children can explore alongside an adult.

## Growing with educator feedback

This is an early preview. Educational content is awaiting Montessori educator review, and the virtual activities complement hands-on experiences with real materials.

If you are a Montessori educator, parent, or school, your observations can help shape what comes next. [Share feedback or suggest an improvement](https://github.com/mrueda/montessori-virtual-room/issues).
